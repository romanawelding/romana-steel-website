Romana Steel & Welding L.L.C. is a UAE-based steel fabrication and welding company delivering reliable solutions for industrial, commercial and residential projects.

Founded in 2022, we bring together fabrication, welding and installation capabilities to support projects from custom steel components to large-scale structures. We focus on quality workmanship, durable results and dependable delivery.
